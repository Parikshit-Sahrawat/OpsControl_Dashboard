## Mandatory customization and adoption gates

1. Replace party names, addresses, dates, privacy/security/support contacts, contracting authority, and legal notices with verified details. Do not invent an EU representative, DPO, grievance officer, lead authority, or Significant Data Fiduciary status.
2. Select governing law, venue, execution form, employment jurisdictions, lawful consideration, and enforceable local restrictive-covenant terms with qualified local counsel. Indian and California post-employment restraints must not be treated as enforceable merely by choosing foreign law.
3. Complete the Order Form's purchased functions, regions, support, capacity, prices, billing, renewal, and retention. Generic SDK, unrestricted OTLP, drag-and-drop dashboards, ML anomaly detection, and complete distributed tracing are not current baseline features.
4. Validate latency and capacity with representative load. The 99.9% SLA is a proposed hosted contractual commitment, not an achieved production result. Define and deploy the measurement and credit process before selling it. A local demo has no hosted uptime undertaking.
5. Implement and validate finite deletion for historical/audit/correlated evidence and backups before adopting the proposed hosted privacy/DPA deletion schedule. Existing append-only controls must not be disabled casually to satisfy deletion. Legal holds require a lawful, narrow, reviewed basis.
6. Complete the real processing, storage/access, subprocessor, cookie/storage, and transfer inventories. A provider connector does not prove that provider hosts this platform. Test requests, rights, breach handling, transfer safeguards, and optional tracking consent. Do not claim SOC 2 certification from a security checklist or shield graphic.
7. Verify current legal text and commencement, especially India's Act/rules/notifications, relevant California regulations, and local employee-invention/non-compete law. Official source sites were not accessible from this environment; these templates do not claim current-law verification or global compliance certification.
8. Obtain valid contributor assignments and a complete dependency/asset provenance review before representing proprietary ownership or granting a commercial license. No license file was identified in the repository inspection; absence is not proof of ownership or permission to relicense third-party work.
9. Complete and attach the mandatory official international-transfer clause texts and actual annexes where applicable. Incorporation references do not replace a complete transfer execution package. Confirm module selection and current UK/Swiss/local requirements.
10. Obtain legal, privacy, security, finance, product, and authorized executive approval. Remove internal template/publication notices only after the applicable gates are satisfied, then publish or execute through the approved process. Keep a versioned acceptance and processing record.
