# Architecture governance and decision register

## Authority precedence and protected boundaries

Controlling sources: [ARCH-ETL-001 V1.6](../../../architecture/ARCH_ETL_001_V16.md) (owner-approved consolidated reconstruction, not an original verbatim transcript) and [ARCH-001 V1.6](../../../ARCH_001_V16.md). [ETL provenance](../../../architecture/ARCH_ETL_001_V16_R1_RECONCILIATION.md), [Sprint 09 report](../sprint09/REPORT.md), [Sprint 10 report](../sprint10/REPORT.md) and the [Sprint 10 architecture matrix](../sprint10/ARCHITECTURE_TRACEABILITY.md) supply implementation and controlled-verification evidence. The proposed [phase-two ADRs](../../../sdlc/phase2/07-ADRS.md) explicitly say PROPOSED and do not amend the approved baseline. New planning tables here carry no approval authority.

The following are unchanged: Resource Management owns canonical configuration/inventory; Data Pipelines, Compute Instances and API Endpoints are operational views; customer Organizations are external monitored entities and Departments are internal employees; shared resources retain native/canonical identities and customer-safe authorization; each canonical collection unit has one fenced active owner with method-specific identity; logs/metrics/traces stay distinct; native platform classifications and all nine version-aware adapter profiles remain; Pentaho is read-only monitoring; native attempts, retry proof, immutable evidence review, timezone/DST schedules, historical incidents and verified recovery rules remain; selected scope grants nothing; legal holds block unsafe deletion. No source recovered in this sprint authorizes any change to these decisions.
