# OC-IMP V2.0 — proposed requirements baseline

This is the owner-authorized **creation of a proposal baseline**, not approval of any individual requirement or replacement of [ARCH-001 V1.6](../../../ARCH_001_V16.md) or [ARCH-ETL-001 V1.6](../../../architecture/ARCH_ETL_001_V16.md). All 62 historical IMP definitions and their approval record remain **SOURCE_NOT_RECOVERED** in the [Sprint 11 register](../sprint11/IMPROVEMENT_REGISTER.md); the eight known titles do not supply missing original descriptions. Original IMP overlap is therefore unresolved for every row below. Stable `OC-IMP` identifiers identify *proposals only*; revisions require a review record, and IDs must not be recycled. Approval state for **all 18 rows: PROPOSED_OWNER_REVIEW**. No implementation is authorized by this table.

Source keys: `NEW-nn` refers to the owner-provided [candidate list](../sprint11/NEW_IMPROVEMENT_RECONCILIATION.md); `P0/P1/P2` refers to the [readiness register](PRODUCTION_READINESS.md); `AUD` refers to [Sprint 10 audit evidence](../sprint10/CODE_AUDIT_FINDINGS.md). `GOV` refers to [owner decisions](APPROVED_OWNER_DECISIONS.md). An existing control named below is overlap, not proof of the full proposal.

`OC-IMP-018` groups related governance **for proposal tracking only**; implementation would require separately reviewed bounded increments. It does not assert the missing historical IMP-055–062 requirements are duplicates. Scope authority, legal holds, immutable evidence, native Pentaho identity, monitoring-only operation and eight other adapters remain controlling constraints. Original-source recovery and individual proposal approval are open [owner inputs](REPORT.md#owner-input-and-approval-queue).
